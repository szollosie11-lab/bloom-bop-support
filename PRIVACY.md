# BLOOM BOP! Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

BLOOM BOP! is published by Erik Szöllösi for a general audience aged 13 and over.

## Offline play and saved progress

The game works offline. Levels, scores, currencies, upgrades, language and sound preferences are stored locally on your device. No account, cloud save, in-app purchase or publisher-operated analytics service is used. Removing the app normally removes its local progress; device backups may retain app data according to your Apple settings.

## Optional Unity Ads

The iOS app integrates Unity Ads. The SDK is initialized only after you enable ads in the game's settings. You can continue playing without ads, and can disable future ad requests in those settings. Internet access is needed for ads.

The app requests contextual advertising, sets Unity's non-behavioral and opt-out flags, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or supply a custom player identifier. Contextual advertising still involves data processing by the advertising provider.

Unity may process device and installation identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device configuration, ad views and interactions, usage and performance information, and purchase-related signals described in its SDK disclosures. Data is used to deliver and measure advertising, operate the SDK and prevent fraud. A report submitted through an ad may include support information. Unity and its partners may process data internationally. See [Unity's game-player privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) and [Unity's iOS SDK data disclosures](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/ads-ios/4.19.0/privacy/developer-consent/disclosures/apple-privacy-survey) for provider practices and privacy choices.

## Contact and choices

For game support or privacy questions, email szollosi.e11@gmail.com. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

To remove local game progress, delete the app and manage any device backups through Apple's settings. For data held by Unity, use the privacy controls in Unity ads and the rights-request instructions in Unity's privacy policy. Turning ads off stops new ad requests from this app; it does not itself delete provider records.

We will update this page if the game's data practices change.
