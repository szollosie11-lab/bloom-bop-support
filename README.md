# BLOOM BOP! Support

BLOOM BOP! is an offline garden defense game for iPhone, with 100 levels, 10 worlds and 10 languages.

For help, email **szollosi.e11@gmail.com**. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a description of the issue. Do not include passwords.

## Playing

Tap PLAY, pick a level and start the battle. Choose a flower card, then tap a safe garden tile. Collect energy, upgrade your helpers and watch for jumping monsters. Contact with a monster removes a helper immediately.

## Saved progress

Progress is saved automatically on your device. Deleting the app can remove progress. There is no account or cloud synchronization.

## Advertisements

Unity Ads initialize automatically. Interstitial ads may appear between completed levels (every fourth completed campaign level, no more than once per two minutes). Rewarded ads are voluntary and grant a bonus only after the provider confirms the reward. There is no switch to disable all ads. Offline play remains available when ads cannot load.
